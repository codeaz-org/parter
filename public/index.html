What it does

Furnish to scale

Drag about fifty pieces onto the plan: sofas, beds, kitchens, bathrooms. Turn, resize and mirror them. Everything is in real centimetres.

Draw or change the walls

Draw walls, add windows and doors, name the rooms. Floor areas are worked out for you.

Start from a drawing

Upload a photo or scan of a floor plan. Trace over it, or let the plan reader turn it into walls and rooms for you to check.

Ask for changes in words

“Put the sofa on the opposite wall.” The assistant edits the plan, and one Undo takes it back.

Walk through in 3D

Stand in any room at eye level, or lift the roof off and look at the whole home from above.

Pictures of the finished room

Turn a 3D view into a realistic picture in the look you choose, or save the views and use them in any image tool.

How it works

  1. Open the editor. You get a sample house. Start your own plan from a picture, an empty page or a file.
  2. Furnish and look around. Move things until the rooms work, then check them in 3D.
  3. Keep and share. Sign in to save plans to an account, open them on any device and send a link to someone else.
