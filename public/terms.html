AI features

The assistant, the plan reader and renders are produced by AI models from other companies. Their results can be wrong. A plan read from a drawing can have wrong sizes, and a render is an illustration, not a promise of how a room will look. Check every measurement against your own drawings before you buy, build or order anything.

Each account includes a number of assistant requests and renders per month. The current numbers are shown on the price section and in your account. Unused allowance does not carry over.
