Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Parter at parter.codeaz.org is run by Anton Zaharia, who is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@codeaz.org.

If you do not sign in

Your plans and pictures stay in your own browser and are not sent to us. If you add your own AI key in Settings, it is stored in your browser and your requests go straight from your browser to the provider you chose. We do not see the key or the requests.

If you sign in, we keep

  • Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google, to identify your account and send sign-in links.
  • Your plans and pictures: the plans, scans you upload and renders you make.
  • Usage counts: how many assistant requests and renders you used this month, to apply the allowance.
  • Payment reference: if you buy Pro, a customer number from Stripe and whether your plan is active. We never see or store your card details.

We use this to provide the service you asked for and, for payments, to meet our legal duties. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use your plans to train AI models.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and a short-lived one protects sign-in with Google. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and Parter uses no analytics service.

Companies that process data for us

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, the database, the stored pictures and sends sign-in emails. Like any host it handles your IP address and keeps short-term technical logs.
  • Anthropic runs the included assistant and plan reader. When you use them, your message, a description of the plan and any plan picture you asked it to read are sent to Anthropic.
  • OpenAI or Google make the included renders. When you ask for one, the 3D view of your room and the prompt are sent to them.
  • Stripe handles payment for Pro and receives your email and payment details.
  • Google, if you choose “Continue with Google”, tells us your email and name. The pages also load their typeface from Google Fonts, which shows Google your IP address.

Some of these companies process data in the United States, under the safeguards they publish for transfers from Europe.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting a plan removes it and its pictures. Account → Delete account removes your account, plans, pictures and usage counts from our systems straight away. Sign-in links expire after 20 minutes. Records of payments are kept by Stripe for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app, save any plan as a file (Export → Project file), and delete your account yourself. You may also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it or to stop using it, by writing to hello@codeaz.org. If you live in the EU or the UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so here and, if you have an account, by email.
